## Research objective

The programme explores how a modular, competition-grade line-following robot platform can be developed using accessible components while maintaining a disciplined engineering process suitable for benchmarking, iteration and future research.

The long-term direction is broader than building a single robot. The platform may evolve into a family of robotics products, while technical findings from design and benchmarking may support research publications where the evidence is strong enough.

## Current stage — Gen-0

The programme is deliberately stage-gated. Gen-0 focuses on establishing reliable foundations before any advanced platform design is attempted.

Current priorities include:

- component procurement and availability;
- 3D-printed fabrication and mechanical fit checks;
- safe electrical assembly;
- individual sensor, actuator and controller testing;
- basic CAD and manufacturability learning; and
- documentation of observations, limitations and test conditions.

The programme will not advance to Gen-1 until the Gen-0 assembly and individual-component-testing milestone is complete.

## Research direction

Future stages are expected to examine sensing architecture, control behaviour, mechanical configuration, calibration, repeatability and competition performance.

Comparative claims will be introduced only after controlled test procedures and benchmark tracks are established.

## Product-development perspective

Alongside the research objective, the programme is being treated as an exercise in prototype-to-product thinking: modularity, serviceability, fabrication repeatability, component availability and documentation are considered from the beginning rather than added after the robot works.
